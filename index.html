The Platform

Everything you need from a weather app.
Nothing you don’t.

Clearcast takes data from the National Weather Service and presents it the way it should be — organized, readable, and designed for quick decisions.

🌤

Clean Forecast Dashboard

Current conditions, hourly breakdowns, and a 7-day extended forecast — all in a single, scannable interface with no visual noise.

📡

Live Radar & Map

Built-in radar view with NEXRAD precipitation overlay on OpenStreetMap. Centered on your location, updated in real time.

⚠️

Real-Time Alerts

Active weather alerts displayed with severity, timing, and expandable detail. Clear, actionable information when conditions change.

Capabilities

Built for depth. Designed for clarity.

Clearcast uses progressive disclosure to keep the interface clean by default, while giving you full access to detailed weather data whenever you need it.

📈

Hourly & Daily Breakdowns

Drill into any hour or day for temperature trends, precipitation chances, wind speed, humidity, and detailed NWS narratives.

🌡️

Advanced Conditions

Expand current conditions to see dew point, pressure, visibility, wind gusts, sunrise and sunset times, and feels-like temperature.

📱

Responsive Design

Fully responsive from mobile to desktop. Horizontal-scroll hourly cards, collapsible sections, and a radar that works on any screen size.

🔍

Smart Location Search

Search by ZIP code, city and state, or coordinates. Autocomplete suggestions, recent searches, and favorites for quick access.

🌓

Dark & Light Modes

Toggle between dark and light themes with one click. System preference detection included. Your choice persists across sessions.

⚡

Lightweight & Fast

No heavy frameworks. Vanilla JS, minimal dependencies, intelligent caching. Runs comfortably on a Raspberry Pi with 4GB of RAM.
